## What you can do

eggz works as an **app inside your AI assistant**. Drop a receipt or bill into ChatGPT or Claude. Your assistant reads it, eggz assigns a category to every line, and you get an editable card. Save it and it goes straight into your eggz account, with no switching tabs and no typing it in.

<Note>You can try it **without an eggz account**. Extraction runs as a guest preview; your bills are only saved once you sign in.</Note>

## Connect eggz to your assistant

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your assistant's connectors or apps">
    In **ChatGPT**, open **Settings → Apps & Connectors**. In **Claude**, open **Settings → Connectors**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add eggz">
    Choose **eggz** from the directory, or add it by its connector URL if you're an early-access user. (eggz is rolling out to the ChatGPT app and Claude connector directories.)
  </Step>
  <Step title="You're ready">
    No sign-in is needed to start — you're only asked to sign in when you save your first bill.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Capture a bill

<Steps>
  <Step title="Share your document">
    Ask eggz to capture a bill and attach the file. ChatGPT or Claude reads the document itself and hands eggz the details it found, so the file types and sizes it accepts are your assistant's, not eggz's. You can send up to **10 bills** in one capture.
  </Step>
  <Step title="eggz categorises">
    Your assistant reads the supplier, dates, line items, amounts and tax. eggz assigns an **expense category** to each line from its fixed list and recalculates the line totals, subtotal and total.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and edit the card">
    An interactive card appears. Edit the supplier, reference, issue date, due date, descriptions and amounts, change any **category**, expand a bill to review its lines, or remove one. Totals update as you go. A supplier, an issue date and at least one line item are required. Everything else is optional.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save to eggz">
    Select **Save to eggz**. If you're not signed in yet, the button reads **Sign in to save**. Sign in (or sign up in one tap) and your bills save. When they have, the card says *Your bills have been saved to eggz.* and shows an **Open in eggz** link to **[Expenses](/help/guides/expenses)**. If the save fails, the card offers **Try again**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>Capturing several invoices? Upload them together and review them as a batch — each bill keeps its own categories and totals.</Tip>

## What the card shows

| Field | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| Supplier | Name (and tax/ABN if shown on the document) |
| Reference | Optional |
| Dates | Issue date (required) and due date |
| Line items | Description, quantity, unit price, and line total |
| Category | Auto-assigned per line; fully editable |
| Totals | Subtotal, tax/GST, and total, recalculated as you edit |

<Warning>Reading a document isn't perfect. Always review the card before saving, especially totals on faded or photographed receipts.</Warning>

## Where your bills go

Saved bills land in **[Expenses](/help/guides/expenses)** in the eggz web app, ready to publish to **[Xero](/help/guides/xero-sync)** like any other document. The categories you choose map to your Xero accounts when you reconcile.

<Note>
Prefer to capture bills straight from your browser? The free **[eggz Chrome extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/eggz-tab-manager-bill-cap/gfjdjhpcemmakhoaggnihfcohkmfcfcc)** enters this same capture pipeline and consumes the same **document processing credits** as dashboard uploads. It takes a single bill off the page you are reading, or [every bill on a supplier's billing page in one run](/help/unscrambled-tabs/collect-bills). See [Connect your eggz account](/help/unscrambled-tabs/eggz-account).
</Note>

## Privacy

<Note>Documents you share are used only to extract your bill. Nothing is saved to an eggz account until you sign in and choose **Save to eggz**.</Note>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which assistants are supported?">
    ChatGPT and Claude (any host that supports the Model Context Protocol apps/connectors standard). eggz is rolling out to their app and connector directories. The eggz Chrome extension has its own assistant built in, so you do not need a connector for that one: see [AI assistant & the command bar](/help/unscrambled-tabs/ai-and-search).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What file types can I send?">
    That depends on ChatGPT or Claude. They read the file and send eggz the details, so eggz sets no file type or size limit here. Uploading a file in the eggz web app has its own limits: see [document upload](/help/guides/uploading-documents).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need an eggz account?">
    No. Extraction and the editable card work as a guest preview. You only sign in (one tap) when you save, which creates your account if you don't already have one.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I capture more than one bill at once?">
    Yes, up to 10 bills in one capture. Review them as a batch and remove any you don't want before saving.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where do my bills end up?">
    In [Expenses](/help/guides/expenses) in the eggz web app, ready to review and publish to [Xero](/help/guides/xero-sync).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I selected Save but it asked me to sign in">
    That's expected the first time, because saving needs an eggz account. Select **Sign in to save**, sign in (or sign up in one tap), and your reviewed bills save. Your edits on the card are kept.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The bill card didn't appear">
    Make sure the file actually attached, then ask eggz to capture the bill again. If the card still doesn't show, start a fresh message (or reload the conversation) and re-share the document.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="An amount or category looks wrong">
    Edit it directly on the card before saving. Change the supplier, any line, or the **category**, and totals recalculate automatically. A quick review is always worth it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
