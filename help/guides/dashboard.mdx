## Dashboard overview

The dashboard is your home screen in eggz. It gives you a snapshot of your business finances and quick access to upload new documents.

## Analytics widgets

The top of the dashboard shows four summary widgets. Revenue and Monthly Spend show the current month with a six-month sparkline and the change **vs last month**:

| Widget | What it shows | Data source |
|--------|---------------|-------------|
| **Revenue** | Total inbound income | Invoices from Xero |
| **Monthly Spend** | Total outbound expenses, leaving out [drawings, loans and assets](/help/guides/reporting-views#what-spending-leaves-out) | Receipts + Bills from Xero |
| **Expense Breakdown** | Spending by category, leaving out drawings, loans and assets | Categorised receipt and bill line items. Bills without line detail (common for Xero-synced bills) are included as whole documents |
| **Quote Pipeline** | Total value of quotes over the past 6 months, with the change on last month | Quotes from Xero |

On the **Outstanding** view, three cards take new names: Revenue becomes **Owed to you**, Monthly Spend becomes **Bills you need to pay**, and Expense Breakdown becomes **Outstanding by category**. They show how overdue things are instead of months. See [Understanding your three reporting views](/help/guides/reporting-views).

<Tip>This is the default left-to-right order. See [Personalising your dashboard](/help/guides/personalising-dashboard) to rearrange, resize, add, or remove widgets — and widgets keep updating automatically as new data syncs from Xero or as you upload new receipts.</Tip>

### Drill into the numbers

The cards aren't just for looking at. **Click a card — or a specific month, bar, or category on it — to jump straight to the transactions behind that number**, already filtered for you. Not sure what a card is measuring? Each one has a small **?** that explains it in plain English. Once a card has data, it also shows a **Show All Details** link. In Billed and Collected, the link on Revenue, Monthly Spend and the Expense Breakdown lists exactly what that card counts for its month, with **Subtotal**, **GST** and **Total** on every row. If your business isn't registered for GST, the list shows one column of full amounts. In Outstanding, the list has a single **Owed** column.

Categories read like English, too — you'll see **"Software & Subscriptions"**, not `software_subscriptions`.

<Note>Drill-downs follow whichever reporting view you're in (**Billed**, **Collected** or **Outstanding**), so the detail matches the headline number above it. The dashboard opens on your business's accounting method unless you've pinned your own view, and your view choice sticks for the rest of your browser session, so flipping to another page and back doesn't reset it. See [Understanding your three reporting views](/help/guides/reporting-views).</Note>

### Explore charts on your phone

On mobile, tap a chart to see its details in a **tooltip** — tap **Show More** inside the tooltip to open the full report. Tapping the chart itself doesn't navigate anywhere, and only one tooltip shows at a time.

## Purchases to review

If you're an admin, **Purchases to review** lives in **Settings > Integrations**, inside the Email-in card. It lists pairs of documents that might be the same purchase captured twice, typically an invoice and its payment receipt: *"One purchase, two documents: combine an invoice and its payment receipt so it's counted once."*

Each suggestion shows both documents side by side with what they share (same supplier, same amount, and so on). Answer **Yes, same purchase** to combine them, or **No, keep separate** to leave them as two records. When a receipt looks like proof you paid an unpaid invoice, the suggestion says **Proof of payment arrived**, and combining also marks the invoice paid.

It no longer appears on the dashboard. See [Two documents, one purchase](/help/guides/receipts-and-bills#two-documents-one-purchase) for the full picture.

## Upload area

Below the widgets, the **Upload Receipts** card has a drag-and-drop zone for quick uploads. Drop a PDF or image file to start processing immediately. See [Uploading Documents](/help/guides/uploading-documents) for details.

## Recent activity

Beside the upload card, **Recent Activity** ("Processed receipts and updates") lists your latest events, such as processed receipts and sync updates.

## Receipt table

Below the upload card and Recent Activity, a table shows your receipts. Columns include **File name**, **Supplier**, **Transaction date**, **Amount**, **Currency**, **Status**, **Uploaded** and **Size**, and you can change which ones show. Click any row to open the receipt detail view.

## Navigation

### Desktop

The desktop header contains:

| Element | Location | Purpose |
|---------|----------|---------|
| **eggz logo** | Top left | Click to return to the dashboard |
| **Main menu** | Top left, beside the logo | Home, Sales, Expenses, Quotes, Customers, Approvals |
| **Ask eggheadz** | Top right | Opens the assistant. Press ⌘J (Ctrl+J on Windows) anywhere. See [Asking eggheadz](/help/guides/asking-eggheadz) |
| **Dark mode toggle** | Top right | Switch between light and dark themes |
| **Help** | Top right question mark | **Community** (opens the eggz community), **Help Centre**, **Live Chat** |
| **Settings** | Top right gear | General, Billing, Manage Users, Integrations, Automations |
| **Notifications** | Top right bell | Open your notifications. A number on the bell counts the unread ones |
| **User menu** | Top right avatar | My Profile, Sign Out |

The dashboard has no search box in the header. Tables have their own search.

### Notifications

Click the bell to see receipts that are ready, held emails and approval notices. Click a notice to go to the receipt, or to the approvals page for an approval. In the bell's settings you can choose which kinds you get: **Receipt Processing** and **Approvals**.

### My Profile

Open the user menu and choose **My Profile**. You can change your name and other personal details and upload a profile picture (JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 5 MB). Click **Save Changes** to keep them. Under **Security**, **Reset Password** emails you a reset link and signs you out everywhere once you reset it.

### Main menu items

| Menu Item | Page | What you will find |
|-----------|------|--------------------|
| **Home** | `/` | Dashboard with analytics, upload area, recent receipts |
| **Sales** | `/invoices` | Sales invoices — synced from Xero and drafts created in eggz |
| **Expenses** | `/expenses` | Receipts and bills in tabbed view |
| **Quotes** | `/quotes` | Quotes — synced from Xero or created in eggz |
| **Customers** | `/accounts` | Customer accounts — synced from Xero or created in eggz |
| **Approvals** | `/automations/approvals` | Actions waiting for a human decision. Shown only to people who see the whole business |

Sales, Expenses, Quotes and Customers each have a **New** shortcut in their menu: **New Invoice**, **New Receipt**, **New Bill**, **New Quote** and **New Customer**.

### Mobile

On mobile devices, eggz uses a simplified layout:

- **Top bar**: Logo, Help, Settings, Ask eggheadz, notifications, user menu, and the main menu button
- **Bottom dock**: Home, Camera (quick capture), Expenses
- **Menu**: The same items as the desktop main menu, including **Home** and **Approvals**, each opening its page with a tap

<Tip>The camera button on the mobile bottom dock opens your device camera to photograph a receipt. Check the photo on the **Review Photo** screen, then it's uploaded and processed.</Tip>

### Settings

Open settings from the gear in the header. Settings has five tabs: **General**, **Billing**, **Users**, **Integrations** and **Automations**. Everyone can open **Integrations**; the other four are for people who can manage settings. See [Settings](/help/guides/settings) for what each tab holds.
