<Warning>
**Engagements and Timesheets are in beta.** They are off for most workspaces. If you don't see them, [ask eggz support](mailto:support@eggz.ai) to switch them on. There is no setting for you to turn on yourself.
</Warning>

## What it does

An **engagement** holds a client's retainer, hourly rate and billing day, so you agree the terms once. **Timesheets** are where you log the hours worked against that retainer and confirm each week. Each billing cycle, eggz works out the invoice from the terms and your confirmed hours, and raises it as a **draft**.

Nothing is sent to your client, and nothing reaches Xero, until you publish the invoice yourself.

## Open them

Once your workspace has the beta, **Timesheets** is in the top menu. Engagements isn't in the menu yet, so go straight to the page:

- Engagements: [app.eggz.ai/engagements](https://app.eggz.ai/engagements)
- Timesheets: [app.eggz.ai/timesheets](https://app.eggz.ai/timesheets)

Without the beta, those addresses show a "not found" page.

## Add an engagement

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new engagement">
    On **Engagements**, click **Add your first engagement**. Once you have one, the button reads **New engagement**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the client">
    Choose a **Customer**, then set the **Name on the invoice**, the **Invoice reference prefix** (A to Z, 0 to 9 and hyphens), and the **Status**: **Active**, **Paused** or **Ended**. Every invoice is referenced as the prefix, then the year and month. The customer can't be changed later. A new customer means a new engagement.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the terms">
    Choose **How the client pays**: **For hours: a retainer or an hourly rate**, **A fixed price**, or **Internal work, never invoiced**. For hours, fill in **Retainer hours a month**, choose **GST on the hourly rate** (**Ex GST** adds it on top, and **Inc GST** means the client pays the rate exactly), then the **Hourly rate**, **Currency**, **Payment terms, in days** and **Billing day**, which can be any day from the 1st to the 28th. Pick what happens to **Hours beyond the retainer**: **Bill them at the rate** or **Absorb them**. Tick **Bill the retainer in advance** if you bill ahead, and **Roll unused hours over** to carry hours left in a period into the next.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say where invoices go">
    Add an **Accounts contact** and, if you like, **Copy to** addresses separated by commas, plus any **Notes**. You can't stage an invoice pack without an accounts contact.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create it">
    Click **Create engagement**. To change the terms later, open the engagement and click **Edit terms**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The list shows each client's retainer, rate, billing day and status. A paused or ended engagement can't have a cycle raised until you set it back to **Active**.

## Log and confirm a week

**Timesheets** opens on **List**, with every entry. Group it by day, client or status, or switch to **Calendar** for a month or a year.

- **Log hours** adds an entry: the day, what was done and the hours. The description goes on your client's timesheet.
- Each entry is confirmed or unconfirmed, and billable or not billable. Only confirmed, billable hours go on an invoice.
- **Confirm** and **Edit** sit on each row. Select a row and press C to confirm it, E to edit it, B to switch it between billable and not billable, or Z to undo.
- Open a client to confirm, change or unconfirm their hours for the period. Once the last hour is confirmed, the gold button becomes **Review and send**.
- Once an invoice email has gone, the hours it billed are locked.

<Note>Members can log their own hours, but don't see **Confirm** or **Edit**. You can also review and confirm a week from [ChatGPT or Claude](/help/guides/review-timesheets-in-chatgpt-and-claude).</Note>

## Raise a cycle

Open an engagement and find **Raise a cycle**. Admins and accountants can raise a cycle.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the billing date">
    Set the **Invoice day**. **Extra hours last period** fills in from confirmed timesheets, and you can change it. **Preview timesheet PDF** shows the timesheet that goes with the invoice, with a subtotal for each week. If the engagement sells a block of hours, fill in **Block instalment** with the instalment number to bill.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the invoice">
    eggz shows the invoice it will raise, with its reference, issue and due dates, lines, subtotal ex tax and total.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Raise it">
    Click **Raise draft invoice**. The cycle appears under **Cycles raised**. Raising the same cycle again gives you the invoice you already have, never a second one.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Publish the draft from the invoice, as in [Publish invoices & quotes to Xero](/help/guides/publish-to-xero).

## Authorise in Xero

Once a draft is in Xero, the **In Xero** card on the invoice shows **Authorise in Xero** for an invoice that's a draft or awaiting approval. Anyone whose role lets them approve invoices sees the button.

1. Click **Authorise in Xero**.
2. Read the confirmation, **Authorise this invoice in Xero?** Authorising posts the invoice to your customer's account in Xero and, as the dialog puts it, locks the invoice number. Xero assigned that number when the draft was created. Nothing is emailed to your customer.
3. Click **Authorise in Xero** again to go ahead. You see **Authorised in Xero.**

To email it to your customer, use **Send** on its **Email pack**, or **Review and send** in Timesheets.

## Send the invoice by email

Each row under **Cycles raised** has an **Email pack**. Open it and click **Stage the pack**. eggz writes the subject and body and attaches the invoice PDF. You can **Copy subject** and **Copy body**, and **Attach a timesheet PDF**.

Nothing is sent until you press **Send**. Connect your mailbox once with **Connect Missive**. The email goes from your own Missive mailbox with the invoice and timesheet PDFs, and the hours it bills are then locked. **Refresh the pack** rewrites the wording from the invoice, so adjust the words in your mail client, not here. **Discard** sets an unsent pack aside.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I see Engagements or Timesheets?">
    They're in beta and switched on per workspace. [Email support](mailto:support@eggz.ai) and ask for timesheet billing.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="It says timesheet billing isn't enabled for this account">
    Your workspace doesn't have the beta, or no longer does. [Email support](mailto:support@eggz.ai) to turn it on.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will my client see anything before I publish?">
    Not an invoice. A raised cycle is a draft inside eggz. Nothing is sent, and nothing reaches Xero, until you publish it. Confirming a week is separate: that makes its hours client-visible, but it doesn't send or publish any invoice.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
