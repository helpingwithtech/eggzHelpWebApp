## Welcome to eggz

eggz connects your email to Xero so you can automate invoices, bills, receipts, and more. This guide walks you through your first few minutes -- from signing up to publishing your first document.

<Info>
Sign up at [app.eggz.ai](https://app.eggz.ai) and click **Sign Up**. Invited founding members get a personal welcome with a note from the eggz team. If you would like a personal invite, leave your details at [app.eggz.ai/request-invite](https://app.eggz.ai/request-invite) and we'll be in touch. Want a look first? [Try eggz without an account](/help/guides/try-eggz).
</Info>

## Prerequisites

- A Xero account (free trial works)
- A modern web browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge)
- Optional: a Gmail account, if you want to send bills through the [eggz Nest label](/help/guides/gmail-eggz-nest)

## Sign up and create your workspace

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to [app.eggz.ai](https://app.eggz.ai) and click **Sign Up**. eggz uses secure authentication powered by WorkOS -- you can sign up with your email or an existing Google account.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create your workspace">
    After signing in for the first time, you will be asked to **Set up your workspace**. eggz pre-fills your workspace name and company domain from your email address.

    - **Workspace name**: Your company or team name
    - **Company domain**: Extracted from your email (e.g. `acme.com`)

    Click **Create workspace** to continue.

    <Tip>Each workspace is a separate organisation. If you work with multiple businesses, you can create a workspace for each one.</Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you were invited, your account and workspace are already set up. Sign in with the **same email address your invite was sent to**, using **Continue with Google** or any of the other sign-in options on the screen.

## Your first ten minutes: the welcome

Every new workspace opens on the same welcome. A bar along the top shows five stops: **Welcome**, **Accounting method**, **Connect**, **Reveal** and **Done**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add your first receipt">
    The welcome greets you with "G'day" and your first name. If you were invited, it also shows your business name and a personal note from the eggz team. Drop in a receipt -- a PDF or a photo -- and eggz starts reading it straight away. You can drop up to 3 documents. If you drop more, eggz takes the first 3 and tells you.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick your accounting method">
    While eggz reads your receipt, the welcome asks one question about your books: **How does your business count income and spending?**

    - **Money in and out (Cash):** a sale counts when you're paid, a bill when you pay it.
    - **Invoices and bills (Accrual):** a sale counts when you invoice it, a bill when you get it.

    Cash is already picked, so **Continue** keeps it, and so does skipping setup. Once you've answered, the welcome doesn't ask again. You can change it any time under **Accounting method** in **Settings > General**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect Xero">
    The **Now connect Xero** screen has one button, **Connect Xero**. Sign in to Xero, authorise eggz, and pick your organisation if you have more than one.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review and publish">
    By the time Xero is connected, your receipt is usually ready -- merchant, total, and line items extracted. Check the details, then click **Approve & publish to Xero**. A box opens right there where you confirm what goes into Xero.

    If eggz couldn't read a receipt with confidence, you see **This one needs your review** instead. Click **Review & publish to Xero** and the receipt opens in a new tab so you can check it yourself. Nothing is lost.

    <Note>**Nothing is written to Xero without your approval.** eggz extracts and prepares your documents, but you always review and approve before anything is published to your books.</Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="See your pipeline">
    The last screen says **Your pipeline is live**. It lists three ways to send eggz the rest of your paperwork:

    - **Chrome extension:** grab receipts and bills straight from your inbox and supplier portals. See the [Chrome extension](/help/unscrambled-tabs/index).
    - **Drag & drop:** anything on your desktop, right into eggz.
    - **Email-in:** forward receipts to your eggz inbox. Pick your address with **Claim address**. See [Email-in](/help/guides/email-in).

    If you dropped more than one receipt, the screen also tells you where the others are. They are saved in eggz, and you can publish them any time from your receipts list.

    Click **Go to dashboard** to finish.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Claim a founding place

The last screen can also show a card headed **Claim a founding place**. Founding members get annual plans from A$90, normally A$190, and the card shows how many of the 100 places are left. Click **Add a card**. Your card claims one, and you pay nothing now. When it works you see **Founding place claimed.** and you get the founding price on any annual plan. Click **Not now** to leave it for later.

Only an admin can add a card. Everyone else is told to ask an admin to claim the place. See [Settings](/help/guides/settings) for plans and billing.

<Tip>Need to get to your dashboard before you've finished? Use the **Skip setup for now — go to dashboard** link. It shows on every step before the last. Connect Xero any time from **Settings > Integrations**, and upload receipts from the dashboard.</Tip>

Gmail is not part of the welcome. To add it, go to **Settings > Integrations**. It works through the [eggz Nest label](/help/guides/gmail-eggz-nest): eggz reads only the emails you put under that label. Plan changes live in **Settings > Billing**.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I can't connect to Xero">
    Make sure you are signed in to the correct Xero account. If you have multiple Xero organisations, you will be asked to choose which one to connect. If the connection fails, try disconnecting and reconnecting from **Settings > Integrations**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I skipped the welcome -- how do I go back?">
    Everything the welcome sets up is in Settings. Change your accounting method under **Accounting method** in **Settings > General**. Connect Xero, claim your Email-in address or add Gmail in **Settings > Integrations**. To publish a receipt, open it from your receipts list.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My workspace name is wrong">
    Go to **Settings > General** to update your **Organisation Name**, timezone, reporting currency, and industry.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
