## From an eggz draft to a Xero draft

Drafted an invoice or quote in eggz — perhaps one the AI assistant put together for you? You can push it straight to Xero as a draft, ready to review, approve, and send from there. Xero assigns the invoice number and keeps your eggz reference, so the two stay linked with no duplicates.

## Publish a draft

On a draft invoice or quote created in eggz, the detail page shows a **Publish to Xero** card.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the draft">
    Open the invoice or quote you drafted in eggz. Publishing works on **drafts created in eggz** that have a customer, valid dates, and a positive total.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read what happens">
    The card explains it up front: *"Publishes a draft to Xero — nothing is sent to your customer. Review, approve and send it in Xero."* It also notes: *"Xero assigns the next invoice number when the draft is created (deleting it leaves a gap in the sequence); your eggz reference is saved in Xero's Reference field."* (On a quote, the card says **quote number**.) For a quote, it adds *"The quote will be dated \{date\}."* — the date you created the quote in eggz, in your organisation's timezone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish draft to Xero">
    Click **Publish draft to Xero**. eggz creates the draft in Xero and links it to your eggz document. Clicking twice won't create a duplicate.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>Publishing creates a **draft** in Xero only — **nothing is sent to your customer**. You review, approve, and send it from Xero.</Note>

## After you publish

Once published, the card shows **In Xero** with a link that opens the document in Xero. While it is a draft the link reads **View draft in Xero** and the card says *This invoice is a draft in Xero. Review, approve and send it there.* Once it is awaiting approval or authorised, the link reads **View in Xero**, and an authorised invoice says to send it to your customer from Xero. (Drafts can't be emailed from Xero until you approve them there.) Two more things happen:

- **View branded PDF** becomes available, so you can see the document exactly as Xero renders it with your branding. See [Your Xero branding in eggz](/help/guides/xero-branding).
- The document is now **linked**, so future [Xero syncs](/help/guides/xero-sync) update it instead of creating a duplicate.

<Note>A document with line items arrives in Xero as those lines, at the prices you entered before tax. You set the account and tax codes while reviewing in Xero. A document with no line items arrives as a single summary line carrying your eggz reference and total.</Note>

## What you can publish

You can publish **drafts created in eggz** that have a customer, valid dates, and a positive total.

Documents **synced from Xero** can't be pushed back. They already live in Xero, and the card tells you so. Your customer is matched to an existing Xero contact when eggz knows its Xero identity. When it doesn't, publishing an invoice can stop on a **Which Xero contact is this?** card. Choose an existing Xero contact and click **Use this contact and publish**, or choose **Create a new Xero contact**. eggz remembers the choice for that customer, so you are only asked once. If Xero reports an archived or duplicate-name contact, the error explains what to fix in Xero.

<Note>Only admins and accountants see the **Publish to Xero** card, and only when they have the publish permission, which your organisation admin manages. If you don't see the card, ask your admin for access.</Note>

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will my customer see this?">
    No. Publishing creates a **draft** in Xero — nothing is sent to anyone. You review, approve, and send it from Xero when you're ready.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why did Xero skip a number when I deleted the draft?">
    Xero assigns the next invoice number the moment the draft is created, so deleting it leaves a gap in the sequence. That's Xero's numbering at work, not an eggz error.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I publish an invoice that came from Xero?">
    No — a document synced from Xero already lives there, so there's nothing to push back. Only drafts you created in eggz can be published.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which branding theme is used?">
    On plans with custom branding, the theme you pick on the document's **Publish to Xero** card, or your organisation's default if you leave it on **Organisation default**. Other plans don't show the picker, and the document uses your organisation's default or Xero's own. See [Your Xero branding in eggz](/help/guides/xero-branding).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
