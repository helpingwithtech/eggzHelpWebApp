## From an eggz draft to a Xero draft

Drafted an invoice or quote in eggz — perhaps one the AI assistant put together for you? You can push it straight to Xero as a draft, ready to review, approve, and send from there. Xero assigns the invoice number and keeps your eggz reference, so the two stay linked with no duplicates.

## Publish a draft

On a draft invoice or quote created in eggz, the detail page shows a **Publish to Xero** card.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the draft">
    Open the invoice or quote you drafted in eggz. Publishing works on **drafts created in eggz** that have a customer, valid dates, and a positive total.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read what happens">
    The card explains it up front: *"Publishes a draft to Xero — nothing is sent to your customer. Review, approve and send it in Xero."* It also notes: *"Xero assigns the next invoice number when the draft is created (deleting it leaves a gap in the sequence); your eggz reference is saved in Xero's Reference field."* (On a quote, the card says **quote number**.) For a quote, it adds *"The quote will be dated \{date\}."* — the date you created the quote in eggz, in your organisation's timezone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish draft to Xero">
    Click **Publish draft to Xero**. eggz creates the draft in Xero and links it to your eggz document. Clicking twice won't create a duplicate.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>Publishing creates a **draft** in Xero only — **nothing is sent to your customer**. You review, approve, and send it from Xero.</Note>

## After you publish

Once published, the card shows **In Xero** with a link that opens the document in Xero. A quote's card says *This quote is a draft in Xero. Review, approve and send it there.* and its link reads **View draft in Xero**. An invoice's card changes as the invoice moves through Xero:

| In Xero the invoice is | The card says |
|---|---|
| A draft | *This invoice is a draft in Xero. Authorise it here, then send it from Xero.* |
| Awaiting approval | *This invoice is awaiting approval in Xero. Authorise it here, then send it from Xero.* |
| Authorised | *This invoice is authorised in Xero. Send it to your customer from Xero.* |
| Partially paid | *This invoice is partially paid in Xero.* |
| Paid | *This invoice is paid in Xero.* |
| Voided, or deleted in Xero | *This invoice was voided or deleted in Xero.* |

The link reads **View draft in Xero** on a draft and **View in Xero** on every other row.

(Drafts can't be emailed from Xero until you approve them there.) Publishing does two more things:

- **View branded PDF** becomes available, so you can see the document exactly as Xero renders it with your branding. See [Your Xero branding in eggz](/help/guides/xero-branding).
- The document is now **linked**, so future [Xero syncs](/help/guides/xero-sync) update it instead of creating a duplicate.

If Xero's total comes back different from eggz's, the card says so, for example *Xero's total for this invoice is $1,000.00, but eggz's is $1,100.00. Check each line item's account and tax rate in Xero before you approve it.* It goes once Xero's total matches and you [sync from Xero](/help/guides/invoices-and-quotes#syncing-invoices).

<Note>A document with line items arrives in Xero as those lines, at the prices you entered before tax, each with its account and tax rate. A line item without them takes your defaults under [Account mappings](/help/guides/xero-sync#account-mapping). A document with no line items arrives as a single summary line carrying your eggz reference and total, on those defaults. After publishing, you can still change them on an invoice that's a draft in Xero: click **Edit** on the invoice.</Note>

## What you can publish

You can publish **drafts created in eggz** that have a customer, valid dates, and a positive total.

Documents **synced from Xero** can't be published. They already live in Xero, and the card tells you so. A draft invoice in Xero can still be [edited in eggz](/help/guides/invoices-and-quotes#edit-an-invoice), which saves the change in Xero too. Your customer is matched to an existing Xero contact when eggz knows its Xero identity. When it doesn't, publishing an invoice can stop on a **Which Xero contact is this?** card. Choose an existing Xero contact and click **Use this contact and publish**, or choose **Create a new Xero contact**. eggz remembers the choice for that customer, so you are only asked once. If Xero reports an archived or duplicate-name contact, the error explains what to fix in Xero.

<Note>Only admins and accountants see the **Publish to Xero** card, and only when they have the publish permission, which your organisation admin manages. If you don't see the card, ask your admin for access.</Note>

## If publishing stops

Publishing stops when an account or tax rate can't be used, and the card says what to change, for example:

- *Choose a sales account and tax rate on the Integrations page in Settings to publish this.*
- *200 - Sales is no longer in Xero. Choose another sales account on the Integrations page in Settings.*
- *GST on Income is archived in Xero. Choose another tax rate on the Integrations page in Settings.*
- *GST on Expenses can't be used on sales in Xero. Choose another tax rate on the Integrations page in Settings.*
- *This invoice adds 15% GST, but GST on Income in Xero is 10%. Change the GST on this invoice, or the tax rate on the Integrations page in Settings.*

When the problem is a line item's own choice, the message ends with that line item instead, for example *Choose another sales account on line item 2 of this invoice.*

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will my customer see this?">
    No. Publishing creates a **draft** in Xero — nothing is sent to anyone. You review, approve, and send it from Xero when you're ready.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why did Xero skip a number when I deleted the draft?">
    Xero assigns the next invoice number the moment the draft is created, so deleting it leaves a gap in the sequence. That's Xero's numbering at work, not an eggz error.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I publish an invoice that came from Xero?">
    No — a document synced from Xero already lives there, so there's nothing to push back. Only drafts you created in eggz can be published.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which branding theme is used?">
    On plans with custom branding, the theme you pick on the document's **Publish to Xero** card, or your organisation's default if you leave it on **Organisation default**. Other plans don't show the picker, and the document uses your organisation's default or Xero's own. See [Your Xero branding in eggz](/help/guides/xero-branding).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
