## Record a payment

On an approved invoice with money still owing, use **Record payment** to log money you've received. You'll enter:

| Field | What it is |
|-------|-----------|
| **Amount** | How much was paid, up to the remaining balance (the dialog shows **Remaining balance**) |
| **Payment date** | When the payment arrived. It starts on today |
| **Method (optional)** | How it was paid, for your records: Bank transfer, Card, Cash, Cheque or Other |
| **Reference (optional)** | A note, such as a transaction ID |

### Which invoices take a payment

Like Xero, a payment goes against an **Authorised** invoice: one that's been approved, so your customer has been asked to pay it. **Record payment** shows on those, while money is still owing, on the Sales list and on the invoice page.

| Invoice status | Record payment |
|----------------|----------------|
| **Draft** or **Submitted** (awaiting approval) | Not shown. Your customer hasn't been asked to pay yet. Set the status to **Authorised** first. |
| **Authorised** with money owing | Shown |
| **Paid** | Not shown. There's nothing left to pay. |
| **Voided** | Not shown. It's cancelled, so nothing is owed on it. |

If eggz turns a payment down, the dialog says why and what to do instead.

## Full and partial payments

- **Full payment** — pay off the whole remaining balance and the invoice is marked **paid**.
- **Partial payment** — pay part of the balance and the remainder stays **Outstanding**. The portion you recorded shows up in **Collected** for the month it arrived.

<Note>Overpayments aren't supported yet — you can record up to the remaining balance, but not more than what's owed.</Note>

## How a payment feeds your three views

A payment doesn't just mark an invoice paid — it flows into your [reporting views](/help/guides/reporting-views):

| View | What a payment does |
|------|---------------------|
| **Billed (Accrual)** | No change. The invoice already counted once it was approved, in the month it's dated |
| **Collected (Cash)** | The amount you recorded counts in the month the payment arrived, before GST if your business is registered for GST |
| **Outstanding** | The remaining balance drops; if the invoice is now fully paid, it leaves Outstanding entirely |

<Tip>See every payment applied to an invoice — in order, with a running balance — in its [settlement history](/help/guides/invoices-and-quotes#settlement-history) on the invoice detail page.</Tip>
