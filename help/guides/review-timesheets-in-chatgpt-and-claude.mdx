## What you can do

With eggz connected to your assistant, ask for your hours in plain words. eggz shows the week on a card, so you can check it and confirm it without leaving the chat. To connect eggz first, see [Capture bills in ChatGPT & Claude](/help/guides/ai-bill-capture#connect-eggz-to-your-assistant).

## Review a week

Ask something like "Show my timesheet for last week" or "Review the hours for this week".

- eggz shows the most recently finished week unless you name another.
- If your organisation bills more than one client, say which one.
- The card lists the **Hours logged** for the week. A week with none shows **No hours logged**.

Reviewing only reads. Nothing changes until you confirm.

## Confirm a week

On the card, click **Confirm week**. Confirming locks the week and makes its hours billable and client-visible. It doesn't raise, publish or send an invoice; those are separate steps in [Engagements & timesheets](/help/guides/engagements-and-timesheets). If the week is already confirmed, the card says so.

<Note>Only an admin can confirm a week. Anyone else sees "Only an admin can confirm a week." Confirming again on a confirmed week is safe: it changes nothing.</Note>

When it's done, the card gives you **Open in eggz** to see the week on the [Timesheets page](/help/guides/engagements-and-timesheets#log-and-confirm-a-week).
