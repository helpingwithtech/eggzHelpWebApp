## Accessing settings

Click the gear icon in the header to open the Settings menu. It lists **General**, **Billing**, **Manage Users**, **Integrations** and **Automations**. These are the five tabs of Settings, and **Manage Users** opens the **Users** tab.

Admins use every tab. Everyone else can open only **Integrations**, where each person connects their own Gmail and sees their held emails.

## General settings

Configure your workspace basics:

| Setting | What it controls |
|---------|-----------------|
| **Organisation Name** | Your company or team name displayed throughout eggz |
| **Timezone** | Affects date display and reporting periods |
| **Reporting Currency** | Default currency for analytics and dashboards. It locks once you have financial data, so contact support to change it |
| **Accounting method** | How your business counts income and spending: **Money in and out (Cash)** or **Invoices and bills (Accrual)** |
| **Financial year starts** | The month your financial year begins, used when you ask about this financial year. Connecting Xero sets it for you; after that, change it in Xero, then refresh in **Integrations** |
| **Industry** | Helps AI categorise expenses more accurately |
| **Default Tax Label** | The tax name used by default on invoices, bills and quotes, such as GST |
| **Default Tax Rate (%)** | Pre-filled on new documents. You can change it on any document |

### Accounting method

Pick the way your business keeps its books:

- **Money in and out (Cash):** a sale counts when you're paid, a bill when you pay it.
- **Invoices and bills (Accrual):** a sale counts when you invoice it, a bill when you get it.

Your dashboard opens on this method, and eggheadz uses it to answer questions about spending and profit. A business that hasn't picked one counts on **Money in and out (Cash)**. You can change it any time; click **Save changes** after you do.

Each person can pin their own view on the dashboard instead. A pin changes what that person sees and how eggheadz counts for them; this setting stays as it is. See [Understanding your three reporting views](/help/guides/reporting-views).

In the Chrome extension, once you're signed in, **Settings > eggz account** has an **Accounting method** row. It opens **Settings > General** in the eggz web app, in a new tab.

## Billing

View and manage your subscription:

- See your current plan and usage: documents and AI messages both count against your plan. The **Document usage** card shows how many documents are left, where they came from and when they reset
- Pick **Monthly** or **Annual** under **Available Plans**, then upgrade or downgrade. Billing opens on the plan you hold, smaller plans say **Downgrade**, and every plan change shows the amount before you agree. You can't downgrade while you're out of documents
- Turn on **Auto top-up** to buy a [document pack](/help/guides/uploading-documents#document-packs) whenever fewer than 5 documents are left. Switch on **Top up documents automatically** and set **Documents per top-up**. It isn't offered on Scale, where documents are unlimited; if it was on when you moved to Scale, Billing keeps the switch so you can turn it off.
- Click **Open billing portal** to manage payment methods and see invoices

### Plans and prices {#plans-and-prices}

| Plan | Monthly | Yearly | Founding member, yearly |
|------|---------|--------|-------------------------|
| **Free** | A$0 | — | — |
| **Solo** | A$19 | A$190 | A$90 |
| **Growth** | A$79 | A$790 | A$390 |
| **Scale** | A$590 | A$5,990 | A$2,990 |

Founding member prices are for annual plans only. The first 100 businesses to put a card on file get a place, and the billing page and the confirm box show your saving. The **Yearly** column is the regular price.

### Plan limits {#plan-limits}

| Limit | Free | Solo | Growth | Scale |
|-------|------|------|--------|-------|
| **Documents** | 50, once | 100 a month | 500 a month | Unlimited |
| **AI messages a month** | 100 | 1,000 | 5,000 | Unlimited |
| **AI messages a day, per person** | 150 | 150 | 150 | 500 |
| **AI messages a minute, per person** | 10 | 10 | 20 | 40 |
| **Quotes and invoices the assistant drafts a day, per person** | 20 | 20 | 20 | 60 |
| **Characters in one message** | 4,000 | 4,000 | 4,000 | 12,000 |
| **Connected platforms** | 2 | 2 | 5 | Unlimited |
| **Team members** | 1 | 1 | 5 | Unlimited |

- **Documents** refill every month on Solo and Growth, whether you are billed monthly or yearly. Free's 50 documents are granted once and do not refill. See [Document limits](/help/guides/uploading-documents#document-limits).
- **AI messages** are shared by your workspace each month, and each person can send up to the daily number. When the month's messages run out, eggz offers an upgrade first. If you'd rather not upgrade, the assistant pauses until your allowance resets.
- **AI messages a minute** count any 60 seconds. Send more than that and the chat tells you how many seconds until you can ask again.
- **A message over your plan's characters** isn't sent. Your words stay in the box, so you can shorten it or split it in two.
- **Connected platforms** are the accounts you connect, such as Xero and Gmail.
- **Team members** are enforced: once your team reaches your plan's number, counting invites not yet accepted, the next invite is refused. Nobody already on your team is removed.

## Users

Manage your team:

- Click **Invite a team member**, fill in an email and a role, then click **Send invite**. The person gets an email to create their account or sign in. If your team is full, the dialog says so and offers **Invite more team members**
- Find people with **Search by name or email** or **Filter by role**
- See each person's role, their status (**Invited**, **Invite expired** or **Invite cancelled**) and when they last signed in, or **Not signed in yet**
- Use **Change role** then **Save role** to move someone to another role
- Use **Resend invite** or **Cancel invite** on an invite that hasn't been accepted
- Use **Remove** for people who no longer need access. Each of these asks you to confirm
- Click **Show more** to page through a big team

Only admins open this tab.

### Roles

The **Role** list in the invite box shows the roles your workspace offers. Under the list, eggz says: "Receipts from the Expenses role need an admin or accountant to approve them." Four kinds of role matter, and what each can do is below.

| Role | What it can do |
|------|------------|
| An admin | Full access to all data, settings, integrations, and user management |
| An accountant | Sees every record in the workspace, but isn't an admin. Can't manage settings, billing, or users. Can approve and send back team receipts |
| A member | Sees only what they created. Can't manage settings, billing, or users, but connects their own Gmail on **Integrations** |
| The Expenses role | Sees only what they created. Their receipts wait for an admin or accountant to approve them before they go further |

If someone holds two roles, the wider view wins.

## Automations

**Automations** is the fifth tab. It lists your workspace's rules, each with a status switch, and a gallery of templates you can add in one click. The **Approvals** button opens the things waiting for a person to decide. Only admins open this tab.

## Integrations

Every integration is one card: its name, one line saying how it stands, and the one button you need right now. Rarer actions sit behind **⋯** at the top right of a card.

### Email-in

- **Copy** your workspace's eggz address
- **Trusted senders**: click to open the list and add or remove senders
- **⋯ > Change address** to pick a new address
- **Emails held back** and **Purchases to review** show on the card while there are some

See [Upload receipts to Xero by email](/help/guides/email-in).

### Gmail

- **Connect Gmail**, then add the **eggz Nest** label to an email in Gmail and it lands in eggz as a pending receipt
- **⋯ > Manage connection** to disconnect Gmail

See [Send emails to eggz with the eggz Nest label](/help/guides/gmail-eggz-nest).

### Xero

- **Connect** (or **Reconnect**) your Xero organisation
- **Xero settings**: click to open **Synced data** (refresh customers, chart of accounts, invoices, bills, quotes and your financial year), **Account mappings** and **Branding themes**
- **⋯ > Disconnect Xero**, after you confirm

See [Xero Integration](/help/guides/xero-sync) for the full setup guide.

### Unscrambled Tabs

- **Add to Chrome** installs our free Chrome extension

<Tip>Xero and Gmail can be set up during onboarding or at any time from this settings page.</Tip>
