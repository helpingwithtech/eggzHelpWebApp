## Accessing settings

Click the gear icon in the top-right corner of the header to open Settings. Settings are organised into four tabs.

## General settings

Configure your workspace basics:

| Setting | What it controls |
|---------|-----------------|
| **Organisation Name** | Your company or team name displayed throughout eggz |
| **Timezone** | Affects date display and reporting periods |
| **Reporting Currency** | Default currency for analytics and dashboards. It locks once you have financial data, so contact support to change it |
| **Accounting method** | How your business counts income and spending: **Money in and out (Cash)** or **Invoices and bills (Accrual)** |
| **Financial year starts** | The month your financial year begins, used when you ask about this financial year. Connecting Xero sets it for you; after that, change it in Xero, then refresh in **Integrations** |
| **Industry** | Helps AI categorise expenses more accurately |
| **Default Tax Label** | The tax name used by default on invoices, bills and quotes, such as GST |
| **Default Tax Rate (%)** | Pre-filled on new documents. You can change it on any document |

### Accounting method

Pick the way your business keeps its books:

- **Money in and out (Cash):** a sale counts when you're paid, a bill when you pay it.
- **Invoices and bills (Accrual):** a sale counts when you invoice it, a bill when you get it.

Your dashboard opens on this method, and eggheadz uses it to answer questions about spending and profit. A business that hasn't picked one counts on **Money in and out (Cash)**. You can change it any time; click **Save changes** after you do.

Each person can pin their own view on the dashboard instead. A pin changes what that person sees and how eggheadz counts for them; this setting stays as it is. See [Understanding your three reporting views](/help/guides/reporting-views).

In the Chrome extension, once you're signed in, **Settings > eggz account** has an **Accounting method** row. It opens **Settings > General** in the eggz web app, in a new tab.

## Billing

View and manage your subscription:

- See your current plan and usage: documents and AI messages both count against your plan
- Upgrade or downgrade your plan
- Turn on **Auto top-up** to buy a [document pack](/help/guides/uploading-documents#document-packs) whenever fewer than 5 documents are left (not on Scale, where documents are unlimited)
- Manage payment methods

### Plans and prices {#plans-and-prices}

| Plan | Monthly | Yearly | Founding member, yearly |
|------|---------|--------|-------------------------|
| **Free** | A$0 | — | — |
| **Solo** | A$19 | A$190 | A$90 |
| **Growth** | A$79 | A$790 | A$390 |
| **Business** | A$190 | A$1,990 | A$990 |
| **Scale** | A$590 | A$5,990 | A$2,990 |

Founding member prices are yearly only, for the first 100 founding members. The **Yearly** column is the regular price.

### Plan limits {#plan-limits}

| Limit | Free | Solo | Growth | Business | Scale |
|-------|------|------|--------|----------|-------|
| **Documents** | 50, once | 100 a month | 500 a month | 1,000 a month | Unlimited |
| **AI messages a month** | 100 | 1,000 | 5,000 | 12,500 | Unlimited |
| **AI messages a day, per person** | 150 | 150 | 150 | 500 | 500 |
| **Connected platforms** | 2 | 2 | 5 | 10 | Unlimited |
| **Team members** | 1 | 1 | 5 | 5 | Unlimited |

- **Documents** refill every month on Solo, Growth and Business, whether you are billed monthly or yearly. Free's 50 documents are granted once and do not refill. See [Document limits](/help/guides/uploading-documents#document-limits).
- **AI messages** are shared by your workspace each month, and each person can send up to the daily number. When the month's messages run out, eggz offers an upgrade first. If you'd rather not upgrade, the assistant pauses until your allowance resets.
- **Connected platforms** are the accounts you connect, such as Xero and Gmail.
- **Team members** are enforced: a workspace can't have more people than its plan allows.

## Users

Manage your team:

- Invite new members to your workspace by email
- View current team members and their roles
- Remove members who no longer need access

<Note>User management is powered by WorkOS. Invited users receive an email to create their account or sign in with an existing one.</Note>

### Roles

| Role | Permissions |
|------|------------|
| **Admin** | Full access to all data, settings, integrations, and user management |
| **Member** | Access to own data only. Cannot manage settings, billing, or users. |

## Integrations

Connect and manage external services:

### Xero

- **Connect/disconnect** your Xero account
- **Sync data** manually: Customers, Chart of Accounts, Invoices, Bills, Quotes
- **Map accounts**: Assign eggz expense categories to Xero account codes

See [Xero Integration](/help/guides/xero-sync) for the full setup guide.

### Gmail

- **Connect/disconnect** your Gmail account using WorkOS Pipes
- When connected, eggz can detect invoices, bills, and receipts in your inbox

<Tip>Both integrations can be set up during onboarding or at any time from this settings page.</Tip>
