## How document upload works

eggz uses AI to automatically extract data from your receipts and bills. Upload a document, and eggz reads the merchant name, date, amounts, line items, and categories -- no manual data entry needed.

## Supported file types

| Format | Extensions | Notes |
|--------|-----------|-------|
| PDF | `.pdf` | Single and multi-page supported. A multi-page PDF is one upload and uses one document credit. |
| JPEG | `.jpg`, `.jpeg` | Photos of receipts work well |
| PNG | `.png` | Screenshots and scanned documents |
| WebP | `.webp` | Compressed web images |
| GIF | `.gif` | Still images only |
| HEIC | `.heic` | iPhone photos, read as they are |

Any file up to 25 MB.

<Warning>Word documents, Excel files and TIFFs are not supported. Convert them to PDF or JPEG before uploading.</Warning>

## Upload methods

### Drag and drop (desktop)

The fastest way to upload on desktop:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to the upload area">
    The drag-and-drop zone appears on the **Dashboard** (home page) and the **Expenses** page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Drag files from your computer">
    Drag one or more files from Finder or Explorer onto the upload zone. The zone highlights when files are hovering over it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Drop to upload">
    Release the files. eggz immediately starts processing each document.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>You can upload multiple files at once. Each file is processed independently.</Tip>

### File picker (desktop and mobile)

Press **Select files** in the upload zone to open your system file picker. Select one or more files and confirm. The button shows how many documents you have left, for example **Select files (104 left)**.

### Camera capture (mobile)

On mobile, use the camera button in the bottom navigation dock:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap the camera button">
    The camera icon sits between Home and Expenses in the mobile bottom dock.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Take a photo">
    Point your camera at a receipt or bill and tap the shutter button.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Retake or submit">
    The photo shows on screen. Tap **Retake** to try again, or **Submit** to send it. A message tells you what happened to it, for example that it's in and the eggheadz are reading it, or that eggz already has it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the camera is blocked or can't start, eggz shows **Upload from Photos Instead** so you can pick an existing image from your gallery.

### From the eggz Chrome extension {#from-the-unscrambled-tabs-extension}

If you use the **eggz Chrome extension** with a [connected eggz account](/help/unscrambled-tabs/eggz-account), you can upload without opening the web app:

- **Upload photos or files** in the **+** menu of the side panel chat — PDF, JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF or HEIC up to 25 MB.
- **Take a screenshot** in the same menu — captures the page you're viewing (for example an invoice in webmail) and uploads it as an image.
- **File the bill on this page** — a task for a page that holds a bill. A PDF is sent as the original file, not a picture. See [Capture bills from any page](/help/unscrambled-tabs/capture-bills).
- **Collect a whole billing page** — three or more documents on one page becomes a single approval, and anything eggz already holds is skipped. See [Collect every bill on a page](/help/unscrambled-tabs/collect-bills).

Extension uploads use the **same document credits and pipeline** as the dashboard. Files appear in eggz like any other upload, and extraction starts automatically.

<Tip>Don't have it yet? [Add the eggz Chrome extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/eggz-tab-manager-bill-cap/gfjdjhpcemmakhoaggnihfcohkmfcfcc). It's free, and it can even [capture bills from any page](/help/unscrambled-tabs/capture-bills) you're browsing.</Tip>

### Email forwarding

Forward a receipt or bill to your workspace's own eggz address and each attachment arrives as a pending receipt automatically — no app to open. See [Email-in: forward receipts to eggz](/help/guides/email-in) for how to get your address and who's allowed to send.

## What happens after upload

<Steps>
  <Step title="Document stored">
    The file is securely stored in eggz and a new receipt record is created. It shows in **Receipts** at once, with status **Reading**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="AI extraction">
    eggz uses AI (powered by Claude) to read the document and extract:

    - Merchant/vendor name
    - Transaction date
    - Total amount and currency (a receipt that shows only "$" is read in your organisation's own currency; it's US dollars only when it says US$ or USD)
    - Individual line items with quantities and prices
    - Expense category
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review extracted data">
    Once processing completes, the receipt status changes to **Processed**. Open the receipt to review and correct any extracted data before syncing to Xero.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Publishing to Xero

After a receipt is processed, you can publish it to Xero from the **Publish to Xero** card on its detail page — you review the pre-filled details, then approve. eggz creates the appropriate record based on whether the receipt is paid or unpaid:

| Payment State | Xero Record Type | What it creates |
|--------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| **Unpaid** | Bill (ACCPAY) | A purchase invoice in Xero for your supplier |
| **Paid** | Spend money | A bank transaction in Xero. Requires selecting a bank account. |

<Note>Each receipt can only be published to Xero once. Make sure the extracted data is correct before publishing.</Note>

See [Publish a receipt to Xero](/help/guides/expenses#publish-a-receipt-to-xero) for the full walkthrough — when the card appears, choosing accounts, and what happens after publishing.

## Document limits

Your plan sets how many documents eggz processes for you. Your live usage is on **Settings > Billing** in the web app, which shows how many documents you have left and, on Solo, Growth and Business (whether billed monthly or yearly), when the monthly allowance refills.

| Plan | Document allowance | Billed |
|------|--------------------|--------|
| Free | 50 documents, granted once (the allowance does not refill) | Free |
| Solo | 100 documents a month | Monthly or yearly |
| Growth | 500 documents a month | Monthly or yearly |
| Business | 1,000 documents a month | Monthly or yearly |
| Scale | Unlimited | Monthly or yearly |

**Settings > Billing** shows the paid plans with their current prices, and your document usage on every plan. Yearly plans are billed once a year and have the same monthly document allowance as the monthly plan; there is no separate yearly cap. Prices and every other limit, AI messages included, are in [Plans and prices](/help/guides/settings#plans-and-prices).

**Document usage** on **Settings > Billing** leads with the documents you have left, then where they come from: your plan's allowance, packs (yours until used) and repeating packs. When you change plan, it shows the new plan's allowance beside the pack documents you still have. The upload button shows the same number, as **Select files (104 left)**, or **Select files (Unlimited)** on Scale.

### Document packs {#document-packs}

Need more documents than your plan includes? Ask eggheadz how many documents you have left, in the web app or the Chrome extension, and buy a pack from the card it shows. To have one bought for you when you run low, turn on **Auto top-up** in **Settings > Billing**.

Packs are sold on every plan except Scale. Scale's documents are unlimited, so there is no pack to buy.

- **Packs run from 100 to 10,000 documents.** A pack of 100 to 499 documents costs A$0.40 a document. From 500 documents, it's A$0.30 a document.
- **Repeating packs come in blocks of 100 documents**, at A$0.30 a document billed monthly or A$0.15 a document billed yearly.

### How document credits are counted {#how-document-credits-are-counted}

- **One document, one credit.** eggz charges one document credit for each document it processes, however many pages it has. A 3-page PDF is one document and uses one credit, not three. Only documents eggz successfully processes count towards your usage, which is the same rule the **Settings > Billing** usage card states.
- **A file eggz already holds is not charged again.** Drop, pick or capture a file eggz already has, byte for byte, and eggz says *receipt.pdf is already in eggz.* instead of processing it, even when you're out of documents. When the eggz Chrome extension [collects the invoices or receipts listed on a page](/help/unscrambled-tabs/collect-bills), it also recognises documents it already holds from that same website, and the approval card tells you the split before you decide, as *3 new, 51 you already have*. A new copy whose bytes differ, such as a fresh **Send tab screenshot** or a PDF the website makes again, is a new document and counts.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My document failed to process">
    Check that the file is a supported format (PDF, JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF or HEIC), under 25 MB, and that the image is clear and readable. Blurry photos or very low-resolution images may fail to extract correctly. Try re-uploading a clearer version.

    After an upload, eggz tells you what happened to each file:

    - *report.pdf is in. The eggheadz are reading it now.*
    - *report.pdf is already in eggz.*
    - *report.pdf is over 25 MB. Shrink it or split it, then drop it in again.*
    - *report.docx isn't a file type eggz reads yet. PDF, JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF or HEIC work.*
    - *Hmm, report.pdf didn't make it through. The eggheadz have been told and are looking; contact support if it's urgent.*

    While a batch uploads, the zone shows how far along it is, for example *Uploading 2 of 5…*. Several files with the same outcome get one message for the lot.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The extracted data is wrong">
    AI extraction is not perfect. Open the receipt detail page and edit any incorrect fields before publishing to Xero. Common issues include misread dates, incorrect totals on faded receipts, and missing line items on complex multi-page documents.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I've hit my document limit">
    Your plan has a document allowance. If you run out part-way through a drop, the eggheadz panel shows a credits card saying how many of your files are waiting. The waiting files stay in the page. Add documents by upgrading from **Settings > Billing** or buying a [document pack](#document-packs), then press **Collect the remaining N** to send them on. On Solo, Growth and Business (billed monthly or yearly, the allowance refills every month either way), you can also wait for the refill. On Free the allowance does not refill, so upgrading or buying a pack is the way to process more.

    A document that arrives with no documents left, by email for example, is kept as a receipt with the status **Out of documents**. Its page offers **Upgrade your plan**, and **Try again** reads it once you have documents. eggz also reads up to 50 documents a day without using credits, shared between uploads and email.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does a multi-page PDF use more than one credit?">
    No. One document is one document credit, however many pages it has. See [How document credits are counted](#how-document-credits-are-counted).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Am I charged again for a document eggz already has?">
    Not for the same file. eggz recognises a file it already holds, byte for byte, however it arrives, and tells you it's already in eggz. A [collect](/help/unscrambled-tabs/collect-bills) also skips documents eggz already holds from the same website. A new copy whose bytes differ, such as a fresh tab screenshot, is processed as a new document and counts. See [How document credits are counted](#how-document-credits-are-counted).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I can't publish to Xero">
    Ensure your Xero connection is active in **Settings > Integrations**. The receipt must be in **Processed** status before it can be published. If publishing a paid receipt as Spend money, you must select a bank account first.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
