## How eggz works with Xero

eggz syncs your accounting data from Xero so you can view invoices, bills, quotes, and customer information in one place alongside your uploaded receipts. The integration is primarily one-way (Xero to eggz), with the ability to push processed receipts back to Xero — and to publish draft invoices and quotes you create in eggz.

## Connect Xero

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Navigate to **Settings > Integrations** and find the **Xero** card.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Connect">
    Click **Connect** on the **Xero** card. Xero's authorisation page opens.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Authorise access">
    Sign in to Xero (if not already) and grant eggz permission to access your data.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select your organisation">
    If your Xero login has access to more than one organisation, eggz shows a selection screen after you authorise — pick the organisation you want to connect. Only one Xero organisation can be connected per eggz workspace, so choose carefully. (With a single organisation, eggz connects it automatically and skips this step.)
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once connected, the **Xero** card reads **Connected to** and your organisation's name, and eggz pulls in your Xero data. Everything else for Xero sits inside the card, under **Xero settings**: click it to open **Synced data**, **Account mappings** and **Branding themes**. Click it again to close them.

## What syncs from Xero

eggz pulls the following data from your Xero account:

| Data | Direction | Notes |
|------|-----------|-------|
| **Invoices** | Xero to eggz | Sales invoices with status, amounts, dates, customer info |
| **Bills** | Xero to eggz | Purchase invoices (accounts payable) |
| **Quotes** | Xero to eggz | Quotes with prospect, amounts, status |
| **Customers** | Xero to eggz | Contact information synced into your Accounts list |
| **Chart of accounts** | Xero to eggz | Account codes used for expense category mapping |
| **Payments** | Xero to eggz | Payments against invoices and bills, including partial payments and their dates. They come in when you connect. |
| **Bank transactions** | Xero to eggz | Spend Money and Receive Money transactions. They come in when you connect. |
| **Branding themes** | Xero to eggz | Invoice and quote theme names and logos — refresh from [Branding themes](/help/guides/xero-branding) |

Payments and bank transactions keep your [Collected and Outstanding views](/help/guides/reporting-views) accurate — eggz knows not just what was invoiced, but what's actually been paid and when.

<Note>Customers refresh automatically every 8 hours. Everything else refreshes by hand: on the **Xero** card, click **Xero settings**, then click **Refresh** next to **Chart of accounts**, **Invoices**, **Bills**, **Quotes** or **Financial year** under **Synced data**. **Customers** has a **Refresh** too, if you don't want to wait. Payments and bank transactions have no **Refresh**.</Note>

**Financial year** shows the month your Xero year begins, labelled **Starts** (for example, *Starts: April*).

### Sync order

When you first connect, **documents sync first, then payments**. So invoices, bills, and quotes appear before the payments that settle them. If a payment ever lands before its document, eggz links the two automatically once the document arrives.

<Note>eggz checks for deleted payments each time it syncs payments. That happens when you connect, so a payment deleted in Xero later stays in your eggz reports until then.</Note>

## What eggz pushes to Xero

eggz can push processed receipts to Xero in two ways:

| Receipt State | Creates in Xero | Use Case |
|--------------|-----------------|----------|
| **Unpaid** | Bill (ACCPAY) | A supplier invoice you still need to pay |
| **Paid** | Spend Money (Bank Transaction) | An expense already paid, recorded against a bank account |

<Note>Draft invoices and quotes you create in eggz can be [published to Xero](/help/guides/publish-to-xero). Documents synced from Xero can't be pushed back, and customer records created in eggz aren't pushed to Xero on their own. When you publish, eggz creates the supplier or customer in Xero if Xero doesn't already have a match.</Note>

## Account mapping

After syncing your chart of accounts from Xero, you can map eggz expense categories to Xero account codes. This ensures receipts are posted to the correct account when published to Xero.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Xero settings">
    Go to **Settings > Integrations** and click **Xero settings** on the **Xero** card.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Refresh your chart of accounts">
    Under **Synced data**, click **Refresh** next to **Chart of accounts** to pull your Xero account list.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Map categories">
    Under **Account mappings**, click **Add Mapping**, then choose a **Category** and its **Xero Account**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your mappings are listed under **Current Mappings**. When a receipt publishes to Xero, each line uses its category's account. The mapping marked **Default** covers everything unmatched. To make a different mapping the default, use **Set as default** on its row. To remove one, delete it and confirm **Delete this mapping?**

People who can't edit mappings see **Contact an administrator to manage account mappings**.

## Managing your connection

### Connection status

The line under **Xero** on the card says how the connection stands:

| The card says | What it means |
|---------------|---------------|
| **Connected to** your organisation's name | Active connection, ready to sync |
| **Connection expired. Reconnect to keep syncing.** | eggz's access to Xero has lapsed. Click **Reconnect**. |
| **Xero stopped answering. Reconnect to keep syncing.** | The connection has a problem, and the card shows what Xero said. Click **Reconnect**. |
| **Keep your bills, invoices and contacts in step with Xero.** | No Xero organisation is connected. Click **Connect**. |

eggz renews its access to Xero while the workspace is in use. A connection nobody has touched for 60 days reads as expired. If renewing fails, the card says the connection expired. Only admins see the **Xero** card, so only an admin can connect, reconnect or disconnect.

### Disconnect Xero

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the card's menu">
    In **Settings > Integrations**, press **⋯** at the top right of the **Xero** card.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose Disconnect Xero">
    A box asks **Disconnect Xero?** Click **Disconnect Xero** to go ahead, or **Keep it connected** to back out.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Disconnecting **immediately revokes eggz's access at Xero** — the connection is withdrawn on Xero's side straight away, not just removed from eggz — so eggz can no longer read or sync your data. It does **not** delete any data already synced into eggz.

### Switch to a different organisation

Because only one Xero organisation can be connected per workspace, to change which one is linked: [disconnect](#disconnect-xero) the current organisation, then click **Connect** on the **Xero** card again and choose the other organisation on the selection screen.

## Limitations

- **One Xero organisation per workspace**: You cannot connect multiple Xero organisations to the same eggz workspace.
- **One-way sync for most data**: Bills in eggz are **read-only copies synced from Xero** — edit them in Xero. (Publishing a processed receipt can *create a new bill in Xero* — see [What eggz pushes to Xero](#what-eggz-pushes-to-xero) — but existing bills never sync back.) Contact details also sync one way from Xero; the CRM fields you manage on customers in eggz (owner, segment, annual value, and so on) live only in eggz and are never pushed to Xero. Invoices and quotes synced from Xero are one-way too — but a **draft you create in eggz** can be [published to Xero](/help/guides/publish-to-xero).
- **Mostly manual refresh**: Customers refresh by themselves every 8 hours. For invoices, bills, quotes and your chart of accounts, click **Refresh** under **Xero settings**. Payments and bank transactions come in when you connect.
- **No bank reconciliation**: eggz displays financial data but does not perform bank reconciliation. Continue using Xero for reconciliation.
- **Currency conversion**: Analytics convert foreign currency at the daily reference rate for each document's date, not a live rate. To use a different rate for one document, click **Set exchange rate** on it. **Reverted to the daily rate** confirms you went back.
- **Invoices settled by credit notes, overpayments, or prepayments** still show as **Outstanding** in eggz for now, even though Xero considers them settled. This is coming in a follow-up. Invoices settled by an ordinary payment are handled correctly today.
