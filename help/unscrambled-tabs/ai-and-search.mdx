You have forty tabs open and a question that spans two of them: which of these have I not saved anywhere yet? Answering it by hand means reading the whole strip.

This page covers eggheadz, the assistant that can answer questions like that, what it can and cannot see, the search in the ask bar, and the dashboard's command bar that finds your things and runs actions by name.

## One chat

Click into the ask bar at the foot of the side panel and type. The chat opens as a sheet over your list, headed **Chat**. Click **Fold away** to tuck it back and return to the list. There are no modes to switch between. One chat handles your bills, invoices, quotes, accounts and receipts, as well as your open tabs and workspaces.

eggheadz is included with your eggz plan. There is no API key to paste.

<Note>
Sign in to unlock the chat. The easiest path is to [connect your eggz account](/help/unscrambled-tabs/eggz-account). Signed out, the chat shows a **Sign in to eggz** card instead. Once you are in, the chat is covered by your eggz plan, and fair use applies.
</Note>

Replies stream in as they are written, so you are not staring at a blank box. Your conversation is kept with your account, so you can close the browser, come back later, and it is still there.

## Try asking

A new chat opens on **Try asking**: up to four questions about what you have to do. Click one to ask it. The list is the same, in the same words, as in the eggz web app. See [Asking eggheadz](/help/guides/asking-eggheadz).

The questions come from what is waiting for you right now: tasks that are overdue or due today, expenses waiting for your approval, receipts with no date, purchases that look like the same bill, customers who owe money, and emails held back from your books (an admin sees every held email; anyone else sees the ones from their own Gmail). If more is waiting than fits in four, the last question becomes **What's waiting on me?**, and its answer links to all of it. With nothing waiting, the chat says **All clear. Nothing needs you.**

The extension adds one more. If a page that looks like a bill is open in another tab, such as a Stripe invoice, you also see *Is the bill on invoice.stripe.com already in eggz?* It names only the site. The page you are on has its own rows under **On this page**, and **Documents in your open tabs** stays last.

Showing the questions costs nothing. Clicking one asks it like any question you type, so it counts as one of your AI messages. When you open the assistant, it sends the titles and web addresses of your open tabs so eggz can suggest questions. It does not include what is on those pages.

Under an answer, eggheadz offers up to two questions that follow from it. Click one to ask it.

### The ask bar

The ask bar sits at the foot of the side panel and along the bottom of the new-tab dashboard, and it works the same in both. The grey words in an empty ask bar are about you. They read **Try:** and the first question or row the open chat would show, such as *Try: File the bill on this page*. With nothing waiting, they read **Search, or ask eggheadz**. Inside the open chat, the box reads **Ask eggheadz anything or @ to mention**, or **Ask a question about this** when a page is attached.

Click into the empty ask bar and **Recent** appears, with up to four rows. Your last conversation is at the top, named by the first question in it and captioned **Chat** with when it was. Click it to pick up where you left off. **New chat** sits at the right of the **Recent** heading and opens a new, empty chat on **Try asking**. Both work from the keyboard with the arrow keys and Enter.

## It knows what you have open

The chat does not only see the one tab you are staring at. It knows about every open tab in every window, including the ones Chrome has put to sleep to save memory. By default it sees only the names and web addresses, the titles and URLs, so it knows what you have going on without waking anything.

It also knows the names of things in your saved library: your workspaces, folders, and saved links. So you can ask it things like:

- *"Which of my open tabs are about the kitchen reno?"*
- *"Did I already save a workspace for the tax stuff?"*
- *"Round up all my shopping tabs and tuck them into one group."*

<Tip>
Sleeping tabs stay asleep. The chat sees the name and address of a sleeping tab, but it will not wake it up to look, because that would spend the memory you were trying to save.
</Tip>

## It asks before it reads a page {#it-asks-before-it-reads-a-page}

Names and addresses are one thing. Reading what is written on a page is another, so the chat asks first. When it wants to read the contents of a page, or look at one of your records, a permission card appears. It reads *eggheadz want to read this page*, shows the page's site and title, and carries a one-word label, **Reads** or **Acts**, that says whether the request only looks or also does something. Choose **Approve** to let it read this page for this request only, or **Deny** to refuse.

It asks every time it wants to read a page, so you are never handing over a blanket pass. The permission card appears right in the chat, at the moment it is needed.

<Note>
Nothing on a page leaves your browser until you say yes. Until you approve, the chat only ever sees names and web addresses, never the actual words on the page.
</Note>

When you do say yes, the extension reads the page cleanly. It pulls out the real article or content and skips the menus, ads and clutter. And when the assistant writes back, any images in its reply are shown as plain text rather than loaded, so a dodgy page cannot use them to phone home.

### Approvals {#the-approvals-inbox}

Decisions your organisation is waiting on do not live in the chat. They sit in the **To approve** section at the top of the **Tasks** tab, for anyone signed in with an organisation. See [Tasks](/help/unscrambled-tabs/tasks#approvals).

## Organise your tabs with a nudge {#organise-your-tabs-with-a-nudge}

The **Open Tabs** tab has an **Organize** button in its heading, titled *Organize your open tabs with AI*. It opens an **AI Organize** window where the AI sorts your open tabs into sensible groups, across all your windows rather than just the one in front of you. You can then drop those groups straight into a new or existing workspace so they are saved for later.

## Bills on the page you are on

When the page you are on looks like an invoice, a banner offers to save it. See [Capture bills from any page](/help/unscrambled-tabs/capture-bills). When the page is a supplier's billing portal listing three or more documents, you get a card offering to fetch the lot in one run, with the count on it before you decide. See [Collect every bill on a page](/help/unscrambled-tabs/collect-bills).

## When your AI messages run out {#when-you-run-low-on-ai-credits}

Your eggz plan includes a number of AI messages a month for your workspace, and a number each person can send a day. See [Plan limits](/help/guides/settings#plan-limits).

When the month's messages run out, the chat offers an upgrade first. If you'd rather not upgrade, eggheadz pauses until your allowance resets. The card also lets you invite a friend, and when they join, you both get free AI credits.

Two other cards can appear. **Today's AI messages are used up** means you have reached your daily number, and it tells you when to ask again. **That was a lot of messages at once** means you sent too many in a short stretch, and it counts the seconds until you can ask again.

## Search anything {#search-anything-k}

To find a tab, a workspace, a record or a task in a hurry, open search from your keyboard, from any tab:

- Mac: ⌘K
- Windows and Linux: Ctrl+Shift+K

This focuses the ask bar with its search showing. Click into an empty ask bar and **Recent** appears. Start typing and matches appear under kind tabs: **All**, **Tabs**, **Workspaces**, **Tasks**, **Suppliers**, **Customers**, **Bills**, **Invoices**, **Quotes** and **Receipts**. Your tabs, workspaces and tasks come first, under the heading **Tabs, workspaces and tasks**. Press **Enter** on a workspace to switch to it, or on a tab to jump straight to it wherever it is. Use ↑/↓ to move, **Enter** to go and **Esc** to close search.

Archived workspaces stay out of the results on purpose, because search is for what you are working on.

Tabs and workspaces are matched on your computer. Records and tasks are looked up in your eggz account, so they appear only when you are signed in. See [Settings & privacy](/help/unscrambled-tabs/settings-privacy#your-privacy-plainly).

## The command bar {#the-command-bar}

On the dashboard, the same box does something more. As well as finding things, it runs actions by name.

Open it while the dashboard is the tab you are on by pressing **/**, which is quickest, or **⌘K** (**Ctrl+K** on Windows and Linux), or by clicking the **Search** button in the dashboard header. Pressing the global search shortcut while you are on the dashboard opens this rather than the side panel.

Type nothing and you get a list of things you can do. Type a word and it narrows. Commands always sit at the top, above the search results, which here come in four buckets: **Workspaces**, **Open tabs**, **Saved tabs** and **Resources**. **Open tabs** covers every live tab across every window, freshest first. **Saved tabs** covers tabs stored inside your workspaces, including any [custom names you have given them](/help/unscrambled-tabs/manage-tabs#rename-any-tab). **Resources** covers your saved links, with the section they live in. Use ↑/↓ to move, **Enter** to go and **Esc** to close.

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Create workspace** | Name it and capture this window |
| **New folder** | Group workspaces in the sidebar |
| **Switch workspace…** | Closes this set of tabs and opens another |
| **Save this tab to…** | Keeps the page you were on, leaves it open |
| **Move tab to workspace…** | Saves the page and closes it here |
| **Archive workspace…** | Keeps everything, leaves the lists |
| **Suspend other tabs** | Frees their memory, and they reload when clicked |
| **Close duplicate tabs** | Same page open twice, so it keeps one |
| **Toggle sidebar** | Shows or hides the workspace list |
| **Open settings** | Account, backup, shortcuts and more |

A name ending in **…** asks a second question. Pick **Switch workspace…** and it follows up with your workspace list. **Esc** steps back.

A command only appears when it would actually do something. **Close duplicate tabs** stays out of sight unless you really have a duplicate, and the tab commands need a web page to act on. Six are shown at a time, so keep typing to reach the rest.

<Tip>
It matches on more than the exact words. Typing *dup* finds **Close duplicate tabs**, *susp* finds **Suspend other tabs**, and initials work too, so **cw** finds **Create workspace**.
</Tip>

## Ask eggheadz from anywhere

To go straight to the chat:

- Mac: ⌘J
- Windows and Linux: Ctrl+Shift+J

Chrome lists this command as **Ask eggheadz**. It opens the chat and raises a small ask box over it, so you can type at once. Close the box and you are still in the chat.

**⌘I** (**Ctrl+I**) puts the cursor in the ask bar, or in the chat's own box while the chat is open. On the dashboard it is the same chat, without leaving the page.

## If you would rather not use the AI

You can skip eggheadz entirely and the rest of the extension carries on. Workspaces, performance modes, snapshots and backup all work with no AI at all. The AI only taps you on the shoulder for AI-specific things.
